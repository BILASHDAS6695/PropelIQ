# US-023: Patient Arrival & Check-In

## Story

**As a** front-desk staff member,
**I want** to mark patients as arrived when they check in at the clinic,
**So that** providers know their patients are ready and the queue is accurately managed.

## Details

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Epic** | EP-002 |
| **Priority** | High |
| **Story Points** | 2 |
| **MoSCoW** | Must Have |

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Staff can search today's appointments by patient name or appointment ID
- [ ] Staff marks appointment status from "Scheduled" → "Arrived"
- [ ] Arrival timestamp recorded automatically
- [ ] Patient cannot self-check-in (staff only)
- [ ] Arrived patients appear in provider's active queue
- [ ] SignalR notification sent to provider's dashboard when patient arrives
- [ ] If patient arrives >15 min late, flag as "Late Arrival" (visual indicator)
- [ ] Audit log entry for arrival marking

## Edge Cases

- Patient arrives but no appointment found → staff offers walk-in registration
- Staff accidentally marks wrong patient → can undo within 5 minutes (revert to Scheduled)
- Patient marked arrived but leaves before being seen → staff can mark "NoShow"
