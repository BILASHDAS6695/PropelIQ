# US-026: No-Show Tracking & Follow-Up

## Story

**As a** clinic administrator,
**I want** the system to track no-shows and trigger follow-up notifications,
**So that** we can reduce missed appointments and improve patient engagement.

## Details

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Epic** | EP-002 |
| **Priority** | Medium |
| **Story Points** | 2 |
| **MoSCoW** | Should Have |

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Staff can mark appointment as "NoShow" if patient doesn't arrive within 30 min of slot end
- [ ] Hangfire job runs 30 min after slot end: auto-marks unchecked-in appointments as "NoShow"
- [ ] No-show triggers follow-up email to patient ("We missed you, please reschedule")
- [ ] No-show count tracked on patient profile (lifetime + rolling 6-month)
- [ ] Admin report: no-show rate by provider, by day of week, by time slot
- [ ] No-show appointment frees slot only after marking (not auto-freed before)
- [ ] Audit log entry for no-show marking (auto or manual)

## Edge Cases

- Patient arrives after auto-marking → staff can override NoShow → Arrived
- Patient with 3+ no-shows in 6 months → visual flag on their profile
- System clock issue → manual no-show marking always available
