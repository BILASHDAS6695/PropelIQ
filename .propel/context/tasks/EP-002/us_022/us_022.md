# US-022: Appointment Cancellation & Rescheduling

## Story

**As a** patient,
**I want** to cancel or reschedule my appointment,
**So that** I can manage my schedule and free up slots for other patients.

## Details

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Epic** | EP-002 |
| **Priority** | High |
| **Story Points** | 3 |
| **MoSCoW** | Must Have |

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Patient can cancel appointment up to 2 hours before start time
- [ ] Cancellation changes appointment status to "Cancelled" and slot status back to "Available"
- [ ] Cancellation reason required (dropdown: schedule conflict, feeling better, other)
- [ ] Cancellation confirmation email sent to patient
- [ ] Reschedule = cancel + rebook in one flow (preserves visit reason)
- [ ] Reschedule shows available slots (same as booking flow)
- [ ] Staff can cancel any appointment regardless of time restriction
- [ ] Cancelled slots immediately available for other patients (real-time)
- [ ] Audit log entry for cancellation/reschedule with reason

## Edge Cases

- Patient tries to cancel <2 hours before → 400 "Too late to cancel online, please call"
- Patient cancels appointment that's already been marked "Arrived" → 400 "Cannot cancel"
- Reschedule but no slots available → cancel portion not executed
