# US-043: Intake Linking to Appointment

## Story

**As a** patient,
**I want** to access the intake form from my upcoming appointment details,
**So that** I can complete intake ahead of time and reduce wait at the clinic.

## Details

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Epic** | EP-006 |
| **Priority** | Medium |
| **Story Points** | 2 |
| **MoSCoW** | Should Have |

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] "Complete Intake" button appears on upcoming appointment detail (if not yet completed)
- [ ] Intake available 7 days before appointment through 15 minutes after check-in
- [ ] Intake link included in 24-hour reminder email
- [ ] Appointment detail shows intake status: Not Started, In Progress, Completed
- [ ] Staff can see which patients have/haven't completed intake for the day
- [ ] Staff dashboard filter: "Intake Pending" for today's appointments
- [ ] Completed badge shown on appointment card in patient's "My Appointments" list

## Edge Cases

- Intake link accessed after appointment completed → "Intake period has ended" message
- Walk-in patient → staff can trigger intake from check-in flow
- Appointment rescheduled → intake retains link to new appointment
